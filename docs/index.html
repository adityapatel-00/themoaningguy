Give your laptop feelings it never asked for.

A fun desktop app that plays funny sounds when it detects sudden noises, like a slap, or when you plug in a charger, USB, or connect a display or network.

Download
Free, open source, and lives in your menu bar. Works on Mac, Windows, and Linux. It sits in your machine quietly until you slap/sound something.

Features

Slap it

Give your laptop a little slap and it plays a funny sound.

Plug it

Plug in your charger and hear a sound. Pull out a USB stick, hear another one. You pick a sound for each.

Your sounds

The app comes with sounds built in, and you can add your own audio files too. Make as many packs as you like.
